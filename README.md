# Assignment #2 Repository

This repository includes the simulated data for Assignment #2. Fork this repository and add your analysis as described in the canvas assignment.

The csv file for `cohort` in the `raw-data` folder includes 5,000 observations with variables `smoke`, `female`, `age`, `cardiac`, and `cost`.

#Summary of findings
 From this sample of 5000 individuals hospitalized to a cardiac ward, inpatient costs associated with female patients were significantly less (by about 250 USD) compared to male patients when adjusting for patient age, smoking status and presence of cardiac event. From Figure 1, you can see that even as patients age, costs for female patients remain lower compared to male patients. Potential explanations include that female patients received fewer inpatient investigations and cost-incurring care. Another possible explanation is that females had a much lower rate of cardiac events than males (12% vs. 88% in Table 1). By virtue of this, they likely underwent fewer cost-incurring cardiac procedures compared to males, although we would need additional data to confirm. Overall, at the individual-level 250 USD may not seem like a meaningful difference; however, this can certainly compound at the population-level. Additional research investigating the mechanism behind lower costs for female patients is warranted.
